# Design review

Adapted from [Impeccable's skill source](https://github.com/pbakaus/impeccable/blob/main/skill/SKILL.src.md) and [critique reference](https://github.com/pbakaus/impeccable/blob/main/skill/reference/critique.md) (Apache-2.0; [license](https://github.com/pbakaus/impeccable/blob/main/LICENSE), [notice](https://github.com/pbakaus/impeccable/blob/main/NOTICE.md)), and [Taste Skill](https://github.com/Leonxlnx/taste-skill/blob/main/skills/taste-skill/SKILL.md) ([license](https://github.com/Leonxlnx/taste-skill/blob/main/LICENSE)). This concise local adaptation does not include their installers, commands, detector, or hooks.

Start with the audience, primary task, available assets, and product character. Make the visual direction support those constraints, then inspect the rendered page at desktop and narrow widths. Check real content, edges, hierarchy, readable line lengths, focus, interaction states, loading and errors. Fix the largest observed problems and recheck; describe concrete findings and user impact rather than assigning unsupported scores. Test contrast in context and treat generic-pattern detectors as prompts for judgment, not proof. Preserve user direction and content where upstream house style conflicts.

The Whale is a specimen gallery and collage tool: the specimens and mixer must remain legible and prominent. Consult `docs/DESIGN.md` and `docs/SKILL-APPLICATION.md`.
