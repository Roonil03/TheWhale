# Whale artwork

The collection includes Google Noto Emoji artwork (Apache-2.0) and Twemoji artwork (CC BY 4.0, copyright Twitter, Inc. and other contributors), sourced from [iamcal/emoji-data](https://github.com/iamcal/emoji-data). The manifest records versions and source links. See the [Noto Emoji license](https://github.com/googlefonts/noto-emoji/blob/main/LICENSE) and [Twemoji license](https://github.com/jdecked/twemoji/blob/main/LICENSE-GRAPHICS).

Exports combine and modify source images. When sharing a collage, credit its source creators, link to their source and license, and indicate that the artwork was modified. The project license does not replace the artwork licenses.

The primary font is Space Grotesk, copyright 2020 The Space Grotesk Project Authors, under the SIL Open Font License 1.1. Its unmodified Latin variable WOFF2 was retrieved from the official Google Fonts stylesheet on 2026-11-13 and is served locally. The full license is in `public/fonts/OFL-Space-Grotesk.txt`. Mono labels use the system monospace font.

The original line-art whale and raster social preview are generated using `tools/lineart`. They do not contain vendor emoji artwork. Remixing licensed artwork may create attribution or share-alike obligations; a downloaded collage does not grant additional rights to any source artwork.
